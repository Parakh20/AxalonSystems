FAQ

Common questions.

01How long does deployment take?
Each AXA-9 dock is a one-time, one-day install per 15 MW block. Site survey, dock placement, and network handshake take roughly four hours per unit. The first autonomous mission flies the same day.
02Can I try it before committing to hardware?
Yes — our Drone-as-a-Service tier covers a single scheduled inspection of your park at ₹1,000/MW (or ₹1,500–2,000/MW with full AI analytics). You get the same defect report you'd receive from the resident platform, with zero hardware investment.
03Does the drone work without internet at the site?
Yes. Defect classification, mission logs, and trend deltas run entirely on the dock controller. When connectivity returns, the system syncs to your dashboard — the work orders never get stuck waiting for upstream.
04Who owns the inspection data?
You do. The Autonomous Platform option processes and stores everything on your servers. Axalon firmware updates ship signed; thermal imagery, defect maps, and warranty records never traverse our network.
05What happens to component lifetime at 2 missions per month?
Each unit logs around 10 flight hours per year. Against rated lifetimes (motors 3–5 yrs, propellers 300–500 cycles, ESCs 1,000+ hrs), the only scheduled replacement across five years is a single battery swap at year three.
06Is the platform compliant for warranty claims?
Yes — every mission emits IEC TS 62446-3 compliant thermal records, timestamped and GPS-tagged. The output is the documentation manufacturers ask for when you escalate panel-level defect claims.
