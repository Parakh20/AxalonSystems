The status quo

Faults exist. Finding them fast enough is the problem.

On a utility-scale park, late detection lets small faults cascade into module and string-level failures.

Manual thermography is slow

Handheld scans cover a fraction of panels; a full park takes weeks.

Outsourced drones deliver images, not answers

Raw thermal frames with no severity ranking or loss estimate — you do the analysis.

Inspection cadence is too sparse

Two visits a year means faults compound silently between cycles.

No GPS-anchored fault log

Without geo-tagged records, warranty claims and repeat visits both take longer.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does deployment take?
Each AXA-9 dock is a one-time install per block — site survey, dock placement, and network handshake typically finish in a single day.
Can I try it before committing to hardware?
Yes — a Drone-as-a-Service inspection covers a single scheduled flight of your park, returning the same defect report you'd get from a resident dock, with no hardware investment.
Does it work without internet at the site?
Yes. Classification and mission logs run entirely on-device; results sync to your dashboard once connectivity returns.
Who owns the inspection data?
You do. Thermal imagery, defect maps, and warranty records are processed and stored on your infrastructure.
Is the output usable for warranty claims?
Yes — every mission emits IEC 62446-3 compliant, timestamped and GPS-tagged thermal records, the documentation manufacturers typically require.
