Summary

Drone Thermography Inspection for Solar Power Plant {% if capacity_mw != "N/A" %}of {{ capacity_mw }} MW capacity{% endif %} at {{ site_name }} {% if location != "N/A" %}, {{ location }}{% endif %} was carried out by Axalon Systems.

This inspection was carried out to determine defects that may have arisen during manufacture, installation or operation. Axalon Systems used a Visual and Thermal camera equipped on a Drone for quick and effective Thermal Inspection.

The site is marked with {{ total_detections }} defects that include hotspots, bypass-diode failures, hotspots due to shadow, vegetation and soiling. Defective modules can be further tested for a Root Cause Analysis using IV Curve tests to understand the underlying cause leading to these hotspots.

These hotspots were identified and defect types inferred according to the standard IEC 62446-3: 2017 (Photovoltaic (PV) systems — Requirements for testing, documentation and maintenance — Part 3: Photovoltaic modules and plants — Outdoor infrared thermography).

For clear identification of the criticality encountered during the inspection, the following classification on defect impacts is adopted:

Low Impact — 0°C <ΔT <5°C
No actions to be taken.
Medium Impact — 5°C <ΔT <10°C
Check cause and rectify within a reasonable period.
High Impact — ΔT >10°C
Prompt interruption of operation and rectify issue within a reasonable period.
